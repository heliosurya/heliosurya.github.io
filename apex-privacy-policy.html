Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Apex Pragmatika collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you visit https://heliosurya.github.io/ ("the Site"), use the corresponding tool, or otherwise interact with us.

1. Services Offered Through This Site

This site serves as the public face of Apex Pragmatika's fractional CMO practice and tools. The services we offer include:

  • Fractional CMO leadership — build, operate, and transfer an internal marketing P&L engine.
  • Corporate workshops — hands-on marketing sessions for teams — funnel analytics, content strategy, Martech stack reviews, and growth experiments. On-site / face-to-face or virtual.
  • Student and teacher sessions — marketing workshops for students and educators — funnel thinking, the creator economy, AI-assisted marketing, and career pathways in growth.
  • Owner P&L tools — interactive diagnostic calculators (CAC Payback, True RoAS / MER, FCMO scorecard, channel kill-rules).

2. How We Use Your Information

By design, this site collects as little personal data as possible and only what you choose to provide:
